My dearest Jack,

Happy anniversary, baby! How has it only been four months?! I feel like we've been together for so much longer than that. I have so much to say, but it's hard to express how I feel about you, because words aren't enough to describe how much you mean to me.

You have brightened my life. I've mentioned a few times that I was going through a rough time. Mentally, I wasn't doing my best, and I think my biggest blessing was you. You came at such a bittersweet yet perfect time.

I used to spend most of my time trying to distract myself from my thoughts, but once we started hanging out, that slowly started to change. You began taking over my mind until you became the only thought in it, and that always put a smile on my face.

I find myself reminiscing about our coffee dates: I'd feed you pieces of my pastry, we'd try each other's orders, and you'd always have the better drink. You treated me all the time, taking me out to play pool, paying for my food, and always putting me first. It's the first time I've felt truly loved and cared for.

The distance between us has given me time to reflect on our relationship and on how grateful I am to be part of your life. You are the highlight of my day. I love talking to you, kissing you through the phone, and making jokes, but I especially love when we talk about reuniting and our plans for the future. I can't wait to be with you again.

What excites me most is being able to support you in person. I want to be your person, your other half. Closing the distance will make it so much easier to be there for you, to be the person you can trust and talk to about anything. I know we've already established that, but being there for you physically is what I want. I want to be near you and be with you forever and ever.

Thank you for always being patient with me. I know we've had some minor bumps, but I'm glad we have been able to talk and get through them. I'll keep trying my best, learn from my mistakes, and become the best version of myself for you.

It's kind of crazy that you're my boyfriend. You were just Jack from the service desk, who I may have had a little crush on, and now I get to share my life with you. You mean the absolute world to me. I'm so lucky to be your girlfriend.

I love you so much, Jack. I hope to keep making you happy and making you feel loved, because you've made me such a happier person.

Happy four months, baby. Thank you for always being you. I feel so loved and safe with you, and I can't wait to make many more memories together and see what the future has in store for us.

All my love,Genesis
